# Introduction

## What is DX?

DX is an architecture pattern. Not a framework. Not a library. Plain HTML and JavaScript. Little code to write. No extra layer. Nothing to learn beyond HTML and JavaScript. Scales to large frontends.

## The idea behind it

Its rules don't create the problems that later need frameworks, build systems, and always-changing mechanisms. The idea is that today's frontends are over-engineered, and that over-engineering isn't here. The extra complexity is gone, and what's left is what the interface actually does. Even so, what you build with it can still be large, modular, and scalable.

## What the docs cover

First the foundations, the rules everything else builds on. Then the toolkits, Util and Val. Then complete examples to read and run. It ends with the larger topic, scaling.

## Who it's for

DX is for developers who want less code to write and less to maintain. It's also for the people who choose the stack, CTOs and product owners, who often pick a heavy framework because everyone does, not because the project needs it. That choice costs later: slower apps, and more time and money to change and maintain them.
